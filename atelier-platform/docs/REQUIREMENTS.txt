Build a Production-Ready Multi-Tenant Salon Management CRM & Business Operating System
1. Project Overview
Build a complete, modern, production-grade Salon Management CRM & Business Operations Platform designed for hair salons, beauty salons, barbershops, spas, nail studios, skincare clinics, and other beauty service businesses.
The platform should function as an all-in-one business management system that allows salon owners to manage their entire business from one centralized dashboard.
This is not simply an appointment booking application. It must be a comprehensive CRM, employee management system, inventory management system, booking platform, customer engagement platform, marketing automation system, and business analytics solution.
The product must be designed as a scalable SaaS application supporting multiple independent salon businesses (tenants).
Use a temporary professional brand name. Design a complete brand identity, including logo direction, typography, colors, visual language, and UI components. The brand name should be easy to replace later without changing application architecture.

The most important architectural principle is centralized control with tenant-level flexibility.
The Super Admin controls what the platform offers. Individual salon owners control how their enabled modules operate within their own businesses. Employees only access what their assigned permissions allow. Customers only access their own information and authorized booking experiences.
Build the system with security, privacy, readability, usability, maintainability, and scalability as core engineering principles.
